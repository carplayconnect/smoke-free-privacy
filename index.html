Unlit: Quit Smoking Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Your data stays on your phone. Unlit has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage:

  • Your first name, if you choose to add one
  • What you use (cigarettes, roll-your-own, heated tobacco or vape), your quit date, habits, prices and currency
  • The situations you usually smoke or vape in (your triggers)
  • Your motivation and your reasons for quitting
  • Your journal: cravings (time, trigger, how long it lasted) and logged cigarettes
  • Your buddy's name and phone number, if you choose one
  • Your notification preferences

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. The home screen widget reads the same on-device data to show your progress.

Camera

The Panic Button can show your front camera as a mirror. The image is displayed live on your screen only. It is never recorded, saved, analysed or sent anywhere. Camera access is optional; you can deny it or turn it off at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings, and every feature still works.

Contacts

When you choose a buddy, iOS shows its own contact picker. The app receives only the one contact you pick (name and phone number) and never has access to the rest of your address book.

Messages

"Text my buddy" opens Apple's Messages with a suggested text. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send yourself. The app cannot read your messages.

Notifications

Reminders (milestones, daily check-in, money goals) are scheduled locally on your device. No push service or server is involved. You can turn them off in the app or in iPhone Settings.

Tracking and third parties

The app does not track you across apps or websites, does not contain third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not share data with third parties.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase all data in the app, or delete the app. Either option permanently removes everything from your device. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Health information

The recovery timeline is general information based on public guidance from the World Health Organization (WHO) and the NHS. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist for personal advice and stop-smoking support.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
