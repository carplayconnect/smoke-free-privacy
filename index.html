Unlit: Quit Smoking Tracker

Quit smoking or vaping, one craving at a time.

Need help? Email unlit.quitsmoking@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There are no accounts, so nothing is lost if you stay on the same device. Deleting the app deletes your data.

The camera doesn't show

Open iPhone Settings → Unlit → Camera and turn it on. The mirror is never recorded.

I'm not getting reminders

Open iPhone Settings → Unlit → Notifications and allow them. Then check the toggles in Unlit → Settings.

I had a cigarette. Do I start over?

No. Tap Panic → "I smoked" or Settings → "Log a slip". Your total journey keeps counting; your savings are adjusted honestly.

Start again from zero

Settings → Erase all data. This removes everything and shows the welcome screens again.
